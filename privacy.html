Leonida Clips – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Leonida Clips is a private tool that publishes videos to the channel owner's own social media accounts.

1. What we collect. When the owner connects TikTok, we receive the basic account information TikTok shares (account ID, display name and profile picture) and an access token that allows posting videos to that account. We do not collect information about other TikTok users or viewers.

2. How we use it. Only to upload and publish videos to the owner's own account and to check that each upload finished correctly.

3. Storage and security. Tokens are stored on a private server that only the owner can access. They are never sold, shared or used for advertising.

4. Retention and deletion. Data is kept only while the account is connected. Disconnecting the app in TikTok (Settings > Security > Manage app permissions) or writing to leonidaclipscanal@gmail.com deletes it.

5. Children. The app is not directed to anyone under 18.

6. Contact. leonidaclipscanal@gmail.com
